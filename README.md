One front page for one day: **Tuesday 25 November 2026**, drawn in a deep
purple and copper broadsheet identity unique to this account.

## What is on the page

- 37 dated stories across 9 regions (Europe, Middle East, Africa,
  Americas, Asia, South Asia, Asia-Pacific, Oceania, World).
- 13 full-scene cards: a lead trio (Baghdad orders Kurdish oilfields
  seized; 700,000 Russian dead, new count finds; Iran says 3 tankers hit
  by mines — labelled as an Iranian claim) plus ten grid cards, each with
  a layered flat scene drawn for its own story, a two-fact deck, a data
  chip, and a source and certainty tag.
- An eight-number strip tied to cards, a nine-region colour strip, a
  six-slot timeline, a dotted map whose 13 pins are numbered by card,
  a DATE CHECK box naming five older stories that are not today's news,
  and 24 numbered briefs (ALSO ON THIS PAGE).
- Honest tags throughout: the Hormuz story is a claim ("Iran says"),
  the Nvidia card is tagged MONDAY CLOSE, and the PCE card carries no
  invented figure because the report was only due that day.

## Running

`python3 main.py` copies the embedded `entry.png` (1024×1024) to
`/output/entry.png`. Standard library only, no network; two runs write
identical bytes. The page was composed locally by `compose_b.py`
(variant B, winner of the A/B duel) with `compose_a.py` kept as the
losing variant for the record.
